Every one of these choices can heavily bias, change, or determine what comes out of a simulation. And because every simulator makes a different bundle of choices, simulations are difficult to compare, as are their claims to validity.

We cannot deal with all of that at once. So I am starting from the basics: name the axes, isolate one cell, and make the measurement precise.

Start with the first distinction. Agentic simulations give the model memory, tools, goals and an action loop. Non-agentic simulation is one mapping from a user context to a next output. Our work isolates the second, because it is the building block inside the first.

04Time

Single-turn

The second distinction is time. Single-turn evaluation looks at one observed action. Multi-turn evaluation follows a trajectory, with feedback and state. If the single-step generator is wrong, the multi-step world is built on a weak foundation.

05Evaluation A

Plausibility

real / not real
SocialSimBench
Could this user have written it?socialsimbench.com · 05 / 16

Plausibility asks whether a post could have come from this user. SocialSimBench turns that into a Turing-style discrimination task: two posts, one human and one possibly generated, and the player picks the human. This captures the many-worlds side of evaluation.

06Evaluation B

Realisticness

Persona Sweep
How likely is the post the user actually wrote?Sociogram: Martin Grandjean, CC BY-SA 4.0 · 06 / 16

Realisticness is narrower and exact. We take the post the user actually wrote and ask how much probability the model assigns to it, given that user. Plausible alternatives may be good simulations, but they are not the observed behavior. This is the micro-level, single-step measurement.

This is our position: non-agentic and single-turn. Park et al. refers to the 1,052-person survey study, LLM Agents Grounded in Self-Reports Enable General-Purpose Simulation of Individuals. Each response is single-step, but the system adds reflections and retrieval, so it sits closer to a light agent. OASIS, SimUSER, Concordia and Y Social run agents over time. We deliberately isolate the generator underneath those systems.

08The measure

Perplexity

Likelihood of behavior, not a judge’s taste.Gauge: public domain · 08 / 16

The metric is the likelihood of the real post. We report bits per byte so that models with different tokenizers can be compared. At perplexity one the real post is certain; as perplexity grows, the model assigns it less and less probability. This is a proxy for realism, not a measure of semantic quality or stance.

09Experimental axis 1

Personas

What does the model know?
Description, demonstration, or environment?Linnaeus: public domain · 09 / 16

The first experimental axis is the user representation. We separate written descriptions, examples of the user's writing, and platform context. That includes the user's own bio, an LLM-written biography, recent and representative posts, length-matched older posts, and what the user read, reposted and liked.

10Experimental axis 2

Models

Size × training × format × reasoning.10 / 16

The second axis is the model. We sweep size, families, base versus post-trained checkpoints, raw, document and chat formats, and reasoning off versus on before scoring. The important rigor is that the users, posts and metric stay fixed while these factors change.

11Experimental axis 3

Dosed persona context

How much is dose—and how much is this user?11 / 16

We dose the user's own writing from zero to fifty posts. Then the control asks the crucial question: is the gain really about this person, or would another user's posts produce the same improvement? That separates user-specific signal from generic exposure to social-media text.

The design replicates the same measurement across Bluesky and Reddit, and across an old and a contemporary window. Each group contains roughly ten thousand ordinary users and fifty thousand target posts. The point is to stop a result depending on one platform, one year, or one convenience sample.

15The practical answer

Show user
traces.

P3 · LLM biography
posts > descriptions
negative change = persona helps · same model, format and k15 / 16

The practical answer is to show the model what the user wrote. Older own posts are strongest when there is no recent writing in the prompt. Representative posts also help. An LLM-written biography is a substitute when no posts are available, and its benefit largely disappears or reverses once real posts are present.

Final line: show user traces. Descriptions compress a person; traces preserve the behavior we are trying to predict.
